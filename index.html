Travel InformationFrequently Asked Questions

How long is the flight from the UK?

Direct flights from London typically take 9-10 hours, depending on your departure airport and weather conditions. Most flights depart from London Heathrow (LHR) or Gatwick (LGW). Some routes may require one connection, which can extend total travel time to 12-15 hours. Business class offers lie-flat seats that make the long-haul journey much more comfortable.

Which airlines operate this route?

Several airlines offer this route from the UK. Many routes require one connection through US hubs like Miami (MIA), Atlanta (ATL), or New York (JFK). During peak season, some airlines may offer direct flights from London. Compare business class options across different carriers to find the best options.

What is the best time to book?

For the best availability, book 2-3 months in advance for travel during shoulder seasons (November, May, or early June). Peak season (December-April) requires booking 4-6 months ahead for business class. Tuesday and Wednesday departures typically offer better availability than weekend flights. Consider flexible dates when searching to find the best options.

Do UK citizens need a visa?

UK citizens do not need a visa for stays up to 90 days. You'll need a valid passport with at least 6 months validity remaining. All travelers must complete a Jamaica immigration form, which can be done online before travel or on arrival. Check current entry requirements before booking, as regulations can change. Business class passengers often receive expedited immigration processing.

What is Sangster International Airport like for arrivals?

Sangster International Airport (MBJ) is Jamaica's busiest airport and handles most international flights. The airport has modern facilities including duty-free shops, restaurants, and currency exchange. After your journey, you'll find efficient immigration and customs processes. Business class passengers may receive priority handling. The airport is located just 15 minutes from Montego Bay's hotel strip, making transfers quick and convenient.

What are the business class options?

Business class from the UK offers various seating configurations, service levels, and amenities depending on the airline. Options include lie-flat seats, premium dining, lounge access, and enhanced baggage allowances. Premium economy is a middle option for those seeking additional comfort without the full business class experience. Compare multiple airlines to find the best business class options.
