Your ArrivalMontego Bay & Sangster International Airport

Montego Bay is Jamaica's second city and the main entry point for visitors heading to the island's celebrated north coast — a stretch of coastline that takes in Doctor's Cave Beach, the cliffs of Negril further west, and the resort towns of Ocho Rios to the east. Most UK travellers booking flights to Jamaica Montego Bay land at Sangster International Airport (MBJ), Jamaica's busiest airport, just a short transfer from the main hotel strip.

Because most resorts sit within an hour of the airport, a rested arrival in business class makes a genuine difference — you can be poolside on the same afternoon you land, rather than losing the first day of the trip to jet lag.

Timing Your TripBest Time to Fly to Jamaica from the UK

Jamaica's dry season, from December through to April, is the most popular window for UK travellers, with warm days and low rainfall across Montego Bay. If you're comparing flights to Jamaica Montego Bay for this period, it's worth booking business class well ahead, as premium cabins on Caribbean routes fill quickly.

Shoulder Season Advantage

Travelling in November or May often means quieter airports on both ends of the journey, a more relaxed check-in experience, and a gentler introduction to the island before the winter crowds arrive.

Once You LandMaking the Most of Montego Bay

Arriving rested opens up more of the first day. Doctor's Cave Beach and the Hip Strip are minutes from the airport, while the Rose Hall estate and its clifftop golf course sit a short drive east. Further along the coast, Negril's Seven Mile Beach and Ocho Rios' waterfalls make popular day trips for visitors based in Montego Bay.

Good to KnowFrequently Asked Questions

How long is the flight to Montego Bay from the UK?

A direct flight between the UK and Montego Bay typically takes around nine to ten hours, depending on departure airport and prevailing winds.

Which airport do I land at in Montego Bay?

Flights arrive at Sangster International Airport (MBJ), Jamaica's busiest airport and the main gateway for the island's north coast resorts.

What are the benefits of flying business class to Jamaica?

A flat-bed or extra-recline seat, priority check-in and boarding, lounge access before departure, and a quieter, more restful cabin for the long haul.

When is the best time to fly to Montego Bay?

December to April is the most popular window, when Jamaica sees its driest weather. November and May offer a quieter alternative.
